One map for rainfall, stream gauges, forecast, Lake Superior, watersheds and lidar across the TSA3 counties. Data comes from public agency services. Slow-changing regional layers (public waters, impaired waters, easements, trout, karst, culvert surveys, FEMA hazard zones) draw first from monthly snapshots kept in the site and then refresh live; each legend note says which you are seeing. The site stores nothing about you; only the team project list lives in a database.

Reading the map

  • Dots are precipitation stations (CoCoRaHS, COOP, ASOS via RCC-ACIS), colored by the total for the window set at the top. Grey = no report. Hover for the value; click for the station's record.
  • Triangles are stream gauges (MN DNR/MPCA cooperative gaging merged with USGS), colored by today's flow relative to the site's record for this date. Click for hydrographs with percentile bands.
  • Stars are team projects (sign in on the Projects tab).
  • Click anywhere else for a point: ground elevation, the HUCs and land cover it falls in, trails within 100 ft, public waters, impaired waters, FEMA flood zone, MPCA contamination and regulated sites nearby, easements, federally listed species from USFWS IPaC (including northern long-eared and tricolored bat ranges), wetland, parcel, soils, nearby wells, gridded rainfall and percent of normal, forecast, soil moisture, Atlas 14 design storms, and the watershed tools. The chips at the top of the panel jump to each section.

Controls

  • End date / Window: the station colors and all totals follow these. ← → step the date; the ‹ › buttons do the same.
  • Map tools (left edge): zoom, locate me (centers on your GPS position and follows until you drag the map; if the button reports that location is blocked, allow it for this site from the lock icon in the address bar), measure distance or area (click or tap points, then Finish; on a keyboard, double-click or Enter finishes and Esc clears; the distance tool's Profile button charts the lidar ground profile along the line), and compare imagery (a swipe divider between the current basemap and any imagery year). The search box also finds tax parcels by PIN (any county, dashes optional) or by owner name (4+ letters), and takes coordinates: 46.9476, -91.7849, or UTM 15N as metres 592123, 5200456 or US survey feet 1943000, 17062000. Every Point panel shows the spot in lat/lon and UTM 15N metres and US feet with copy buttons.
  • Radar: Now (reflectivity) is the live NWS radar mosaic, the same picture as radar.weather.gov. The Last 1 h … 24 h choices are rain totals estimated from radar and gauges, issued hourly and 1–2 h behind. The legend states which one is on and its valid time.
  • Layers ▾ (grouped Water / Land / Hazards & subsurface): Watersheds (USGS HUC-8/10/12 boundaries, any zoom), Contamination (MPCA cleanup and regulated sites, groundwater plumes, land-use restrictions, closed landfills), Trails (MN DNR, Superior National Forest and OpenStreetMap hiking, bike, ski and snowmobile trails), Land cover (NLCD 2021; the runoff tool's curve number uses it), Public waters (PWI), Impaired waters & TMDLs, Conservation easements (RIM, wetland bank), Wetlands (MN NWI update), Trout streams, Streams (StreamStats grid), Soils (hydrologic soil group or water-table depth), Parcels, FEMA (flood zones and floodways, BFE lines, lettered cross sections, LOMRs), Crossings (DNR culvert surveys plus NBI bridges), Wells (County Well Index, DNR drill holes, observation wells), and Karst. Zoomed out, the regional layers and FEMA hazard zones draw as a simplified whole-region overview and wetlands as the FWS 100 m wetland raster; full detail appears past the zoom shown on each row. Parcels, soils and wells need you to zoom in. Terrain ▾ adds MnTOPO lidar hillshade, elevation color, 2 ft and 10 ft contours, and USGS contours that work at any zoom.
  • Search ( / ): place, gauge, station, project, parcel PIN, owner name or coordinates.
  • Watershed (Point tab): delineates with USGS StreamStats, returns basin characteristics and regression flows, and runs the TSA3 regional curves for bankfull dimensions. Save results to a project.
  • CAD export: draw a rectangle and download imagery, lidar DEM and contours in UTM 15N US feet, with Civil 3D steps.
  • Print site report (Point tab or a project): a one-document hydrology summary with sources.
  • Link copies a URL that reproduces the exact view, date, layers and selection.

Caveats

  • Station values are the 24 h ending the morning of the date shown; today's reports arrive through the morning.
  • Percent of normal is only shown for 7-day and longer windows.
  • PRISM grids lag 1–2 days. USGS and DNR values are provisional. Return periods use the nearest Atlas 14 grid node; use the official PFDS query for submittals.
  • Regression flows take the region at the pour point, without area weighting or gage adjustment. Regional-curve dimensions are planning-level; verify with a bankfull survey. Design-storm peaks in the watershed section use the NRCS unit-hydrograph method with the MSE3 distribution for one lumped, unrouted basin: planning values, checked against MnDOT's HydroCAD example; model subareas and storage for design.
  • Wetland, FEMA, parcel and easement boundaries are inventory or GIS versions. Jurisdictional wetland lines need a delineation, and the effective FIRM, recorded easement and deed govern.
