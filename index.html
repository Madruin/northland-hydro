Northland Hydro · how to use it

One map for rainfall, stream gauges, forecast, Lake Superior, watersheds and lidar across the TSA3 counties. Everything loads live from public agency APIs; nothing is stored except the team project list.

Reading the map

  • Dots are precipitation stations (CoCoRaHS, COOP, ASOS via RCC-ACIS), colored by the total for the window set at the top. Grey = no report. Hover for the value; click for the station's record.
  • Triangles are stream gauges (MN DNR/MPCA cooperative gaging merged with USGS), colored by today's flow relative to the site's record for this date. Click for hydrographs with percentile bands.
  • Stars are team projects (sign in on the Projects tab).
  • Click anywhere else for a point: gridded rainfall and percent of normal, forecast and QPF, soil moisture, Atlas 14 design storms, and the watershed tools.

Controls

  • End date / Window: the station colors and all totals follow these. ← → step the date; the ‹ › buttons do the same.
  • Layers: Stations, radar QPE (NWS estimate for the last 1–24 h), Gauges, and Terrain (MnTOPO lidar hillshade, DEM and contours).
  • Search ( / ): gauge, station, project or place name.
  • Watershed (Point tab): delineates with USGS StreamStats, returns basin characteristics and regression flows, and runs the TSA3 regional curves for bankfull dimensions. Save results to a project.
  • CAD export: draw a rectangle and download imagery, lidar DEM and contours in UTM 15N US feet, with Civil 3D steps.
  • Print site report (Point tab or a project): a one-document hydrology summary with sources.
  • Link copies a URL that reproduces the exact view, date, layers and selection.

Caveats

  • Station values are the 24 h ending the morning of the date shown; today's reports arrive through the morning.
  • Percent of normal is only shown for 7-day and longer windows.
  • PRISM grids lag 1–2 days. USGS and DNR values are provisional. Return periods use the nearest Atlas 14 grid node; use the official PFDS query for submittals.
  • Regression flows take the region at the pour point, without area weighting or gage adjustment. Regional-curve dimensions are planning-level; verify with a bankfull survey.
